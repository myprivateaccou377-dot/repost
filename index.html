What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin (BTC) is a decentralized digital currency created in 2009. It runs on a blockchain, a public ledger with no central authority.

🔐

How to stay safe

Never share your seed phrase. Use hardware wallets for large amounts. Only use verified exchanges. Beware of DMs promising free crypto.

⚡

What is DeFi?

Decentralized Finance (DeFi) lets you lend, borrow, and earn interest without banks — using smart contracts on blockchains like Ethereum.

🪙

Altcoins explained

Any cryptocurrency that isn't Bitcoin is an altcoin. Ethereum, Solana, and BNB are among the largest. Each serves a different purpose.
